## Ready-to-paste prompt

```
Design a social media graphic for Briarrose Gundogs, a boutique gundog training
business in Sevenoaks and the Kent Weald, England. The brand sits in the
"heritage British field sports" world — think Purdey, Holland & Holland,
Schöffel — understated, tasteful, old-money-adjacent, never loud or cartoonish.

BRAND COLOURS (use only these):
- Deep ink green #26301F — primary colour, main accents, primary text
- Soft sage green #5B6350 — secondary/muted text and fine detail lines
- Cream #F3F0E6 — primary background
- Warm stone #EAE6D8 — secondary panel background, subtle depth
- Deep forest green #2C3A28 — for a dark-background version, if needed
- Do not introduce any other colours. No bright colours, no gradients, no neon.

TYPOGRAPHY (describe, don't fabricate substitute text):
- Headline/name treatment: an elegant, classic serif in the style of
  Cormorant Garamond — refined, editorial, a little old-world. Used italic
  for warmth, upright for formality.
- Supporting text: a clean, understated geometric sans-serif in the style of
  Jost, set in small tracked-out (letter-spaced) capitals for labels/taglines.
- IMPORTANT: image models render text poorly and cannot match an exact
  font. Leave clean, uncluttered negative space where headline and caption
  text would go, rather than generating the words yourself. I will overlay
  the real logo and text afterwards in Canva/Photoshop using the exact
  brand fonts.

LOGO / CREST:
- I've attached the brand crest (three real gundogs — a Wirehaired Vizsla
  flanked by two liver-and-white Springer Spaniels — inside a hand-drawn
  briar rose and oak leaf wreath, engraved/linework style, ink-green on
  transparent). Do not redraw, restyle, or reinterpret the crest or the
  dogs. Treat it as a fixed logo asset to be placed cleanly into the
  composition (e.g. centred, or in a corner with breathing room around it),
  not as inspiration to generate new dog artwork from.

IMAGERY STYLE (for any background/scene elements):
- Warm, natural light photography style — English countryside, autumn
  hedgerows, dew, muted greens and browns, gundogs working in the field or
  resting by a Land Rover. Editorial and calm, never staged-looking or
  overly saturated. If dogs appear in a photographic scene (not the crest),
  keep them realistic working gundog breeds (spaniels, HPRs, retrievers) —
  never cartoon, never stylised.

BRAND ATTRIBUTION:
- Include a small, unobtrusive line of space near the bottom of the
  composition for the credit text "Part of Dog Smart Training & Behaviour"
  — modest size, quiet, clearly secondary to the Briarrose Gundogs identity.
  Do not generate this text yourself; just leave clean space for it in the
  same tracked-capitals sans-serif style as other supporting text.

FORMAT: [insert size/aspect ratio from the variants below]

The end result should feel like it belongs on the same shelf as a Purdey or
Holland & Holland Instagram page — quiet confidence, craftsmanship, nothing
try-hard.
```

## Brand reference sheet (for you, or to paste into any other tool)

**Colours**

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary ink green | `#26301F` |
| Soft sage green | `#5B6350` |
| Cream (primary background) | `#F3F0E6` |
| Warm stone (panel background) | `#EAE6D8` |
| Deep forest (dark-mode background) | `#2C3A28` |
| Deep forest, soft variant | `#3E4C37` |

**Fonts**

- Headings / brand name: **Cormorant Garamond** (serif) — weight 500, italic used as the accent style (this is the font baked into the new crest wordmark itself)
- Body / labels / navigation: **Jost** (sans-serif) — weights 400–600, small caps sections are tracked out (extra letter-spacing)

Both are free on Google Fonts, so any designer or tool (Canva included) can pull them by name directly.

**Dog Smart attribution wording**

Use exactly: *"Part of Dog Smart Training & Behaviour"* — small, secondary, never competing with the Briarrose name or crest for attention.

## A few practical notes

1. **Text will still come out wrong if you let the AI generate it.** Even with the instruction above, image models frequently botch small text. The safest workflow: generate the *background and composition only*, then add the real "Briarrose Gundogs", the crest, and the Dog Smart credit line yourself afterwards in Canva (free) using the exact hex codes and font names above.
2. **Attach the crest file** (`briarrose-crest.png`) to the ChatGPT conversation before running the prompt, so it can be placed into the composition rather than reinvented — image models will otherwise draw their own (wrong) version of the dogs.
3. **Keep a consistent aspect ratio per platform** using the Format Variants above — Instagram in particular crops inconsistently if you reuse one size everywhere.
4. **If a post needs caption copy too**, ask ChatGPT separately in plain text (not the image prompt) — something like: *"Write an Instagram caption for Briarrose Gundogs announcing [topic], warm and knowledgeable tone, no emojis, mention we're part of Dog Smart Training & Behaviour once, keep it under 150 words."*
